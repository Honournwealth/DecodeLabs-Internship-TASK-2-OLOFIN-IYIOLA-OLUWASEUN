# 📊 Project 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
## DecodeLabs Internship Program

---

## 👋 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analytics Internship at DecodeLabs**.

The objective was to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a cleaned transactional dataset using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn).

The goal was to extract meaningful insights, identify patterns, and understand customer behavior and sales performance.

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

- Total Rows: **891**
- Total Columns: **14**

### Key Features
- Order ID
- Customer ID
- Product
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Price
- Payment Method
- Order Status
- Coupon Code
- Referral Source
- Date

---

# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

---

## 📈 1. Descriptive Statistics

### Key Observations
- Average Order Value: **1057.64**
- Maximum Order Value: **3456.40**
- Minimum Order Value: **11.39**
- Average Quantity per Order: **2.91**

### 💡 Insight
Customer spending is relatively moderate, with occasional high-value transactions driving revenue spikes.

---

## 📊 2. Distribution Analysis (Histograms)

### Observations
- Most numerical variables show a balanced distribution
- Total Price is slightly right-skewed

### 💡 Insight
A small number of high-value purchases contribute significantly to total revenue.

---

## 📦 3. Outlier Detection (Boxplots)

### Observations
- Outliers exist in Total Price and Unit Price
- Quantity is fairly stable across orders

### 💡 Insight
High-value transactions are rare but significantly impact revenue performance.

---

## 🔗 4. Correlation Analysis (Heatmap)

### Key Findings
- Quantity ↔ Total Price: Strong positive correlation
- Unit Price ↔ Total Price: Strong positive correlation

### 💡 Insight
Revenue is primarily driven by pricing and purchase quantity.

---

## 💳 5. Payment Method Analysis

### Observations
- Certain payment methods dominate transactions
- Others are used less frequently

### 💡 Insight
Customers prefer specific payment channels, indicating opportunity for payment optimization.

---

## 📦 6. Order Status Analysis

### Observations
- Majority of orders are completed successfully
- Minor portion of orders are cancelled or returned

### 💡 Insight
Operational efficiency is strong, but returns should be further analyzed.

---

## 📈 7. Sales Trend Over Time

### Observations
- Sales fluctuate across time periods
- Clear peaks and dips in performance

### 💡 Insight
Sales are influenced by time-based factors such as seasonality or campaigns.

---

## 🛍️ 8. Top Products Analysis

### Observations
- A small number of products dominate sales volume
- Top 10 products contribute significantly to revenue

### 💡 Insight
Sales follow a Pareto distribution (80/20 rule).

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Revenue is concentrated in a small group of products  
- Customer spending is moderately consistent  
- Payment behavior is highly concentrated  
- Sales show clear time-based fluctuations  
- Outliers represent high-value customer segments  

---

# 📌 Recommendations

- Focus marketing on top-performing products  
- Investigate high-value outlier transactions  
- Optimize underused payment methods  
- Leverage seasonal sales trends  
- Improve retention of high-value customers
